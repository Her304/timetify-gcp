# Static Pages / Content Voice

`frontend/src/components/{about,help,community,terms,privacy}/` — marketing/legal pages routed at `/about`, `/help`, `/community`, `/terms`, `/privacy` in `App.jsx`. Not behind auth.

**Voice is per-page, not global — check the file before editing copy:**
- `about.jsx`, `help.jsx`, `community.jsx` — **formal British English** by explicit request, no abbreviations allowed. The only abbreviations allowed is "artificial intelligence" spelled out as "ai", British spellings ("centre", "organising"). Still all-lowercase typographically (that's the brand's type system, unrelated to register — kept even in formal copy). Other marketing/product copy sitewide (e.g. `AddMenu.jsx` hints, onboarding tour) still uses the original casual texting-shorthand voice — only these three pages were converted.
- `terms.jsx`, `privacy.jsx` — formal legal documents in **American legal English** (Delaware governing law, American Arbitration Association, DMCA, CCPA/CPRA, FERPA). Left as-is intentionally — these cite real US jurisdiction/statutes; a wording-only Anglicisation pass wasn't wanted, and changing the underlying jurisdiction is a legal decision, not a copy-editing one.

**Help page** (`help.jsx`) is a categorised FAQ/docs page, not just a contact form: hero → jump-nav pills → `FaqSection`/`FaqItem` accordions (seven categories: getting started, your schedule, friends and availability, events, chat and snaps, account and privacy, ai agents) → contact card. Content lives entirely in `faqData.js`; the pills, the accordions and the prerendered `FAQPage` structured data all derive from that one array, so adding a category needs no `help.jsx` edit. "ai agents" is last deliberately — it mirrors the advanced, opt-in placement of the Settings card it documents (see [AI-Agent Bridge](agent-mcp.md)). `StepShot` component renders a screenshot or a video (`video: true` on the `shot` object) inline; images load from `frontend/public/help/<name>.png` and fall back to a "drop `<name>` here" placeholder via `onError` rather than a broken image, while videos are given a full URL (e.g. a public GCS link) and fall back to a "couldn't load" placeholder instead. Two slots wired up: `how do i add my classes?` plays `https://storage.googleapis.com/timetify-prod-media/help/syllabus-upload.mp4` (uploaded from a screen recording — mux to `.mp4` with `-movflags +faststart` before uploading, `timetify-prod-media` bucket is public-read so the direct URL works unsigned); `can i create an event straight from a chat?` plays `https://storage.googleapis.com/timetify-prod-media/help/event-from-chat.mp4` (same mux/upload recipe).

**Icons on `about.jsx` and `community.jsx`** use Google Material Symbols (Outlined), not `@untitledui/icons` — rendered as `<span className="material-symbols-outlined" style={{ fontSize: 24 }}>icon_name</span>` inside the usual `w-12 h-12` circle, color inherited from the parent's `color` style. Every icon used anywhere in the app must be added to the consolidated `icon_names=` query param on the Material Symbols stylesheet in `frontend/index.html` — Google Fonts subsets the font to just the listed glyphs, so a missing name silently renders blank. **That URL now appears twice and both copies must be kept identical:** the real one (`media="print" onload="this.media='all'"`, so the icon font cannot block first paint) and a `<noscript>` fallback. Do not add a third `<link>`, and do not "fix" the `media="print"` — that attribute is what keeps it off the critical path (see [SEO](seo.md)). `Mail01` on the community page's contact button is still an Untitled UI icon, intentionally unchanged.
